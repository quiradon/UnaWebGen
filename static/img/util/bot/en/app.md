Transform your **tabletop RPG** with convenience and powerful features.
Access tools dedicated to various systems like Dungeons & Dragons (DnD), Pathfinder, and [many more.](https://rpg.arkanus.app/en/) Also, enjoy features such as automatic character sheets, initiative management, dice rolling, magic grimoires, and much more. [Check out the full list of available commands!](https://rpg.arkanus.app/en/commands)

**Dice Rolling:**

Use RPG syntax to roll dice with specific numbers of sides and quantities. For example, `5d20` rolls five 20-sided dice and adds the results.
[Click here](https://rpg.arkanus.app/dices) to learn more about complex dice rolling systems like `4#6d6ei`.

Customize your dice by choosing from 45+ skins available in the store 
`/store dices`.

**Installation and Setup:**
Follow this simple guide to add Mini Kraken to your RPG server:

1. Click "Add to server."
2. Use the command `/config` to define the Master's role (Use the Master's role to view and edit other players' sheets)
3. Done! Start using Mini Kraken in your RPG sessions and don't forget to have fun.
